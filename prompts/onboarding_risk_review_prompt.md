# Onboarding Risk Review Prompt

Use this prompt manually with your AI tool of choice. Do not paste private customer data into a public or unapproved tool.

```text
You are helping a founder review customer onboarding risk.

Context:
- Company:
- Business model:
- Target activation definition:
- Target activation days:
- Review date:

Paste the onboarding health scorecard, founder attention queue, and SLA risks below.

Task:
1. Identify the top onboarding risks.
2. Explain which accounts are stuck after closing.
3. Identify delayed handoffs and missing owners.
4. Identify accounts that have not reached activation.
5. Recommend founder actions for this week.
6. Recommend owner-backed next steps with due timing.
7. Identify process improvements that would reduce recurring risk.

Output format:
- Executive summary
- Top accounts needing founder attention
- SLA and handoff risks
- Activation bottlenecks
- Owner gaps
- Next 7-day action plan
```
