# Founder Intervention Prompt

Use this prompt manually with your AI tool of choice. Do not paste private customer data into a public or unapproved tool.

```text
You are helping a founder decide where to personally intervene in customer onboarding.

Use the pasted founder attention queue and SLA risk list.

Task:
1. Rank the accounts where founder intervention has the highest leverage.
2. For each account, explain the risk reason.
3. Draft a founder action that is specific and customer-facing.
4. Identify what the founder should ask the internal owner before contacting the customer.
5. Identify what the founder should ask the customer sponsor.
6. Suggest due timing.

Output format:
- Founder intervention ranking
- Account-specific action plan
- Internal questions before outreach
- Customer sponsor questions
- Follow-up owner and due date
```
