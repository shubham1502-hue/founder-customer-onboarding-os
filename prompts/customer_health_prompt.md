# Customer Health Prompt

Use this prompt manually with your AI tool of choice. Do not paste private customer data into a public or unapproved tool.

```text
You are helping a founder evaluate customer health during onboarding.

Use the pasted onboarding scorecard and activation matrix.

For each account that is Watch, At risk, or Critical:
1. Explain why the account is not healthy.
2. Identify whether the issue is usage, sentiment, payment, support tickets, owner coverage, training, integration, data migration, or activation criteria.
3. Recommend the next customer-facing action.
4. Recommend the internal owner.
5. Note whether founder intervention is needed.

Output format:
- Health snapshot
- Critical accounts
- At risk accounts
- Watch accounts
- Recommended customer health actions
```
